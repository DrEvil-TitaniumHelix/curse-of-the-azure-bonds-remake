# Known and memorised spells

A spell entry appears once a party member knows it or has it memorised. Spells chosen for study but not yet learned
count as known when they are in the character's spell list.

Range and duration are often a base value plus an amount for each caster level. Some spells have extra rules, so the
table values alone do not describe every result.

Some powers of items and monsters have no spell name. The pedia lists only named spells and does not make up names for
the others.
