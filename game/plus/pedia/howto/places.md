# Places

There are two kinds of place. A 3D map is a 16 by 16 square area that you walk through in the first-person view. A 2D
backdrop is the large picture shown for areas you travel through without the 3D view.

A place appears after the party has entered it. "Where met" on other entries names the data set and block where the
observation was made. The Map page draws the squares you have visited.
